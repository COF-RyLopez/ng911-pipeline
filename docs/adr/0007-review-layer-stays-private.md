# The OSM-derived review layer is private to each opted-in agency and carries no content to copy

The **Review layer** compares an agency's data with Overture's ODbL themes (OSM-based) to flag places to
check. Generated for every county and published, that repeated extraction could add up to a substantial part
of OSM (ODbL 6.2) and bring in share-alike. So it is built only for an opted-in **Package**, given to that
agency alone for its own review (not public use under ODbL 4.4), and left out of the public catalogue and
tiles. Each flag names a place and what to check, never OSM geometry or names to copy in; the agency verifies
against its own records or open imagery, and the resulting edit is recorded as the agency's. The trade-off:
counties that haven't opted in get no review flags, and the public scorecard can't show them.
