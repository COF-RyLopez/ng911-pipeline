# We never speak for an agency

Opting in happens on a public, per-county page through an issue form (or email), and nothing is built
under an agency's domain until someone confirms from an address on that domain. We recommend a license
(CC0, else CC BY 4.0) and show where it's unclear, but we only update a license in our registry or open the
matching OpenAddresses PR after the agency has posted it where we can link to it. Outreach about unclear
licenses goes out by hand from a template, never automatically. The trade-off is speed: an agency that
never posts a license stays out of the public catalogue, but nothing we publish can claim permissions or an
identity the agency didn't grant.
